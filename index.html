How to play

Swing, grip, survive

  • Hold anywhere to grab the vine hook nearest your finger. Each vine has 3 hooks, so you choose the height. Release to fly.
  • Your grip lasts 4 s per hook. A new hook or a cliff landing resets it. Grip Gloves hold 7 s, and swinging on the same vine builds speed.
  • Spitter bugs on the valley floor aim when you are slow. Swing fast (you glow mint) and they lose track of you.
  • Ninjas leap at the bottom of your swing. A white flash means one is about to jump.
  • Grumpy birds puff poison clouds. Wait for them to fade, or fly through a bird fast to knock it out.
  • Energy is your health. Hits, falls and poison take it; energy bolts give it back. The valley gets more dangerous the longer you last.
