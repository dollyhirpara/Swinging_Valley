How to play

Swing, grip, survive

  • ✋Tap a hook to grab it and swing. Each vine has 3 hooks, so you choose the height. Tap the next hook to jump to it, or tap empty sky to let go and fly.
  • 🧤Your grip lasts 4 s per hook: watch the ring around you drain and turn red. A new hook or a cliff landing resets it. Grip Gloves hold 7 s, and swinging on the same vine builds speed.
  • 🐞Spitter bugs on the valley floor aim when you are slow. Swing fast (you glow mint) and they lose track of you.
  • 🥷Ninjas leap at the bottom of your swing. A flash means one is about to jump: be swinging fast and it misses you.
  • 🐦Grumpy birds puff poison clouds. Wait for them to fade, or hunt them: fly through a bird fast to knock it out.
  • 💥Birds are the only enemy you can knock out. Each pays 15 coins; a 2nd or 3rd within 4 s pays ×2, ×3 (up to 45). Bird missions pay extra.
  • ⚡Energy is your health. Hits, falls and poison take it; energy bolts give it back. The valley gets more dangerous the longer you last.
