01The pitch · what, who, why

Stickman Hook proved one-thumb swinging is fun.
We add risk, a reason to come back tomorrow, and money that doesn't spam ads.

What

A side-view endless swinger in a hand-painted ink valley. Tap one of 3 hooks on a vine to grab it and swing; tap the next before your grip slips. Dodge bugs, ninjas and grumpy birds, grab treasure, last as long as you can.

Who

Mobile players aged 16–30 who play in short breaks. India first (sachet prices, festival skins), then global. Fans of Stickman Hook and Subway Surfers who want more than a level counter.

Why

The swing feels great with one tap, and every second has a choice: slow down and get shot, or swing fast and stay hidden. The grip limit creates a pain; Grip Gloves turn it into tomorrow's reward.

The decision every second

Hang

Easy target

Bugs lock on fast, ninjas wait at the bottom of your swing, and your grip slips after 4 s.

Swing

Hard to hit

Above 460 speed bugs lose you and ninja leaps miss; above 480 you can hunt grumpy birds: 15 coins each, ×2/×3 combos, bird missions.

Energy is health, not a timer: no drain for hanging or swinging. Hits, falls and poison take it; only energy bolts give it back; danger rises the longer you last.

02How it plays

Tap to swing. Outrun the bugs, dodge the ninjas, hunt the birds.

One thumb
  • Tap a hook to grab it (big 80-unit touch area)
  • Tap the next before your grip ring empties: 4 s, 7 s with gloves
  • Tap empty sky to let go and fly
Spitter bugs

Outrun

Aim faster the slower you are; red line, then spit (−10). Above 460 speed they lose you.

Ninjas

Dodge

Predict your swing and leap at its low point (−12). Be fast when it arrives and the leap misses.

Grumpy birds · prey

Hunt

Poison clouds (up to −14/s). The only enemy you can kill: 15 coins, ×2/×3 combo, bird missions.

Energy is health: hits, falls (−15) and poison take it, only energy bolts (+12) give it back. Danger rises 2.5% per second survived.

Valley Pass · full game

30 tiers filled by mission stars and seconds survived. Free track + ₹199 premium: pays back ~150 rubies, gloves, and a season skin that never returns. Late buyers claim every tier they already earned.

Power stays small

Upgrades are energy and grip only. Most long-term goals are cosmetic or new places to swing, so skill always matters more than spend.

03 · MetagameSwinging Valley
04Money · rules first, products second

How it makes money without feeling like a cash grab.

  • No offers on day 0. The first session is pure fun.
  • Never interrupt a run. Offers live on results and in the shop.
  • At most one smart offer per session, with the reason shown.
  • Feel it free first: gloves are free on day 1 before any glove offer.
  • Everything that affects play can be earned, just slower.
  • No lives or energy timers. You can always play.
  • Revives cap at 3 per run, so spending in a run has a ceiling.
EarnSpend
CoinsTreasure, bird knockouts (15, combo up to 45), missions, 2× Treasure Run, ad "double coins"Max energy, bubble limit, skins, new valleys, 1st revive
RubiesRare drop (≈1 per 3–4 runs), 2 per daily mission, ad chest, IAPGloves (10), 2nd revive (5), 3rd revive (15), extra Treasure Run (5), trails

Rubies are rare on purpose: a free player earns about one glove a week, so a glove always feels valuable.

04 · MoneySwinging Valley
05AI in the build and in the game

AI wrote the code and ran the playtests. I made every design call.

Built with AI
  • Claude (Claude Code) turned my design notes into a rules-and-numbers spec, then a single-file HTML5 game. All art, icons, music and sound effects are generated in code.
  • Playwright bots (careful, quick, hanger, tapper) played after every change and measured run length, slips, falls, wall hits, knockouts and errors.
  • Bot data drove fixes: dead-hang re-grab 142 → 529 speed, wall hits 24 → <1 per minute, glove swing capped at exactly 620.
  • I playtested on a phone and set direction: the hook, the economy rules, tap controls, gentler falls, two art directions tested, Ink & Paper chosen.
AI in the game
  • Ninjas predict you. They simulate your swing forward, find when it next passes its low point, and time a leap to meet you.
  • Valley Coach. An on-device player model reads each run (slips, falls, damage by source, missed treasure) and gives one tip.
  • Smart offers. The same model picks one relevant offer per session and shows why.
  • Next: an LLM coach voice, AI-tuned difficulty to keep runs at 45–90 s, and valleys generated for each player.

Disclosure: code, art, audio and write-up drafted with Claude from my specs and decisions, then reviewed and edited by me. Assumptions: India-first audience, INR prices, every purchase simulated, targets are pre-test estimates.

05 · AISwinging Valley
06Shipping · test, kill, watch
Test first
  • Is tap-swinging fun in 30 s with no meta? 10 players.
  • Is 4 s grip fair? Slips per run and where people quit.
  • Gloves vs a plain shop (A/B): who comes back on day 1?
  • Do players find the glove and bubble buttons?
Kill or pivot if
  • Swinging is not fun without the meta → kill.
  • Gloves lift D1 by < 5 points vs control → change the hook.
  • Slips are the top quit reason → raise base grip.
  • Payers < 1% after the Starter Pack → rework offers, not prices.
Already changed from a phone test
  • Holding covered the screen → tap controls.
  • Hooks too small for a thumb → bigger hooks, 80-unit tap area.
  • Steep drops when switching → softened catch, terminal fall speed.
  • Unclear what to kill → birds are the only prey.
